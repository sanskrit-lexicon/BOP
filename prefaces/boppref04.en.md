# EXPLANATION OF THE ABBREVIATIONS

A. is 1) Atmanêpadum (middle voice). 2) Arǵuna's return from Indra's heaven. (\*)
Am. is the Amara-Kôscha.
Bh. is the Bhagavad-Gîta.
Bhatt. is the Bhatti-Kâvya.
Bhar. is Bhartrihari (ed. Bohlen).
Br. is the Lament of the Brâhman woman. (\*\*)
Dev. is the Dêvîmâhâtmya (ed. Poley. Berlin, at Ferd. Dümmler's).
Dr. is the Rape of Draupadî (in my book «Diluvium»).
Ghat. is the Ghatakarpara (ed. Dursch. Berlin, at F. Dümmler's).
Gita-G. is the Gîta-Govinda ed. Lassen.
gr. is the *Grammatica critica linguae Sanscritae* (Critical grammar of the Sanskrit language). (\*\*\*)
gr. comp. is the comparative grammar («Vergleichende Grammatik etc.»). The numbers indicate the paragraphs.
H. is the Slaying of Hidimba. (\*\*)
Hib. is Gaelic of the Hibernian (Irish) dialect.
Hit. is the Hitôpadêsa (Bonn ed.)
In. is the Indralôkâgamanam. (\*\*)
K. is Kâśʾinâtha, an Indian grammarian, whose collection of roots Wilkins edited (The radicals of the Sanscrita Language, London 1815). (\*\*\*\*)
Lass. is Lassen's *Anthologia Sanscritica*.
Lith. is Lithuanian.
M̐. is the Matsyôpâkhyâna, i. e. the episode of the deluge.
Mah. is the Mahâ-Bhârata, Calcutta ed.
Man. is the Manu-Sâstra (Laws of Manu).
Mr. is the Mricʾcʾakatî.
N. is Nala (Berlin, at Nicolai's).
P. is 1) Parasmaipadum (active voice). 2) Pâtaliputra (ed. H. Brockhaus. Leipzig 1835.)

---

(\*) In my book «Diluvium», Berlin, at the Dümmler bookshop.

(\*\*) In my book «Ardschuna's Reise zu Indra's Himmel» (Arjuna's Journey to Indra's Heaven). Berlin, at Wilhelm Logier's.

(\*\*\*) Those who use the smaller grammar written in German should consult the synoptic table of the said book (p. 370 sqq.), which exhibits the order of the rules of both books.

(\*\*\*\*) The explanations of the grammarians Kâśʾinâtha and Vôpadêva I have appended only to those roots whose meaning could not be confirmed from passages of the books. Where the definitions of both grammarians are expressed in the same words, I have omitted the sigla.
